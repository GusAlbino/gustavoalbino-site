About me

I've been designing digital products and brands for over 10 years.

I started in graphic design, moved into digital and today I work mostly in product design. I'm a Senior Designer and the Lead Designer of a B2B SaaS platform for health professionals, where I'm responsible for the design system, the page flows and all of the UI and UX, from the MVP to the second version.

My work goes from branding, visual identity and editorial design to data visualization, illustration and comics. I also handle image retouching and curation. It's the sum of many years of practice, and most new projects end up drawing on a bit of each.

My process starts with brainstorming and research. Then I apply design theory, build hypotheses and turn them into a proposal. I take it for approval and, with leadership's feedback, move on to the final version. Every decision has a reason behind it, which makes design critique clearer, mine and the team's.

I take hierarchy, deadlines and delivery seriously. I work well in a team, and when I lead a project I set the standard and stay close until it ships.

Day to day, my work starts from data I gather with AI and from the research I do within design itself. Figma is my main tool, and I build about 90% of my work in it. I also use Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, plus Affinity, Canva, Procreate and Power BI. Claude Code and Codex are essential to my workflow. The design decisions are still mine.

Outside work, one of my hobbies is trail running, which I've done since 2018. I work in English and Portuguese, speak professional French and work remotely.
