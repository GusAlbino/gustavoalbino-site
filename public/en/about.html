About me

I design, and I run mountains.

I design, and I run mountains. For a while now, one has not lived without the other.

It started in 2018, in Switzerland, hiking the trails. At first it was just walking, a way to breathe between one project and the next. But in 2019 I decided to try running in the mountains, and I never stopped. In 2025 I took on my first trail race. In 2026 I decided to go further: more races, getting to know the athletes, the brands, the coaching teams and the community that drives this sport.

As a designer, it has been over 10 years creating brands, digital products and visual experiences. Much of it abroad, between Australia and Switzerland. At some point I realised my two passions could be one and the same.

That's my edge: I don't look at the outdoor world from the outside. I train, I race, I know what it's like to get my fuelling wrong on a climb and learn first-hand what works.

Both sides go into the same work: ten years of strategic design and the experience of someone who trains and races. Looking good is the floor. What counts is building it with the people who are in it.
