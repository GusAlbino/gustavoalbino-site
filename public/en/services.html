Shall we work together?

Every project follows the same path, from our first contact to delivery. Scope, deadline and price are agreed before I start.

  1. 01
    Meeting

    We start with a conversation so I can understand your business, your audience and what your project needs to solve.

  2. 02
    Analysis

    Then I study your market and your references to set the direction of the project.

  3. 03
    Brief

    I send you a brief with the demands, and we confirm the scope and what you expect, together.

  4. 04
    Quote

    You receive my proposal with scope, stages, deadline and price.

  5. 05
    Feedback and negotiation

    You reply, we adjust what is needed and close the deal.

  6. 06
    Deadline

    We agree on the delivery date together, before I begin.

Payment

We negotiate the price according to your project. What I usually do is 50% at the start and 50% on delivery.
