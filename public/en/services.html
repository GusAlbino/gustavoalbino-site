Services

Each service can come alone or combined, depending on where your brand is. From the first sketch to the manual the team uses after I leave.

How I work

From first contact to delivery.

I work per project, with scope, deadline and price agreed before we start. The process is the same for branding, product, website or landing page.

Payment

The price is negotiated according to the project. The usual practice is 50% at the start and 50% on delivery.

  1. 01
    Meeting

    A first conversation to understand the business, the audience and what the project has to solve.

  2. 02
    Analysis

    A study of the client, the market and the references, to set the path.

  3. 03
    Brief

    A brief with the demands goes to the client, to confirm scope and expectations.

  4. 04
    Quote

    A proposal with scope, stages, deadline and price.

  5. 05
    Feedback and negotiation

    The client replies, we adjust what is needed and close the deal.

  6. 06
    Deadline

    The delivery date is agreed together, before work begins.
