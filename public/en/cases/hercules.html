Hercules

Now you know how to become a demigod

Visual identity and a full marketing arsenal for a supplements brand. From the strength of Greek myth to the attitude of a hip-hop logo. Built to arrive shouting.

← Back to cases
CategoryBranding · Sports supplements
DeliverablesLogo, system, Colossus line, newsletter, videos, social
Year2022
RoleArt direction & design
The concept

Run-DMC meets the son of Zeus.

The brief asked for a bold, in-your-face brand. The reference came from two unlikely worlds: the Run-DMC logo (simple, easy to read and to place anywhere) and Hercules, the demigod, son of Zeus and champion of Olympus: a symbol of strength, toughness and relentlessness.

I blended the Run-DMC logo style with the Hercules name to build a brand that communicates instantly and gives the consumer the feeling of becoming something more by using the product.

Hence the slogan, a promise and not a description: “Now you know how to become a demigod”.

The logo

A dumbbell that becomes an “H”.

The brand is built on a single graphic move: two dumbbells cross to form the letter “H” for Hercules. All of it heavy, flat, no halftone. Strength read in a second.

The two red bars top and bottom are the direct Run-DMC quote: they frame the name, set the rhythm and shout the brand from across the room. Red, black and white. Nothing else.

Brand system

One brand, every space.

From the storefront sign to the social avatar, the system delivers the right lockup for each context, always with the same DNA: dumbbell-H, red bars and a heavy outline.

Colour versions

Colour palette

Red, black, white. Period.

Three flat inks and one support. Red is the shout; black is the base; white is the breath. Steel appears only in the Colossus line, for the metallic shine of the labels.

Typography

Condensed, stencil and reading.

A heavy condensed family carries the name; a military stencil signs the Colossus line; a clean grotesque holds the long copy.

The product line

Colossus: four weapons.

The Colossus line gathers the essential bodybuilding supplements. Same label architecture, one colour per product: each tub is recognised on the shelf at a glance.

The brand in the world

From logo to storefront.

The system tested in real application: signage, sign and environment. Positive, negative and relief, always legible from a distance.

Newsletter

The customer newsletter.

A printed customer newsletter, “Issue 5, March 2022”, introduces the Colossus line and doubles as a supplement guide: whey, creatine, glutamine and the ideal training stack.

The brand system runs through the whole editorial: red column rule, condensed headlines and the high-contrast black-and-white photo holding the page.

Promotional videos

The brand in motion.

A launch film for the web and offer reels for stories. The same visual shout in video, from the red sign to “50% OFF”.

Social & campaign

Made for the feed.

Social media pieces and ads that take the Colossus line to the street, always in the tension between the athlete and the brand.

Outcome

A brand that arrives shouting.

From the icon to the launch film, one cohesive system: red, black and white, heavy outline and the same attitude across every medium.

More than a logo: a complete brand, with product line, editorial and video, ready to become a demigod on the shelf.
