The first version

The first mark was the monogram alone: a G and an A built in quadrants inside a ring, signed GADSG, for Gustavo Albino Design. It worked as a watermark and as an avatar, and it stopped there.

It lived on colour. Magenta, green, a purple to cyan gradient, negative on black. Four variants of one drawing, which is comfortable to make and expensive to keep: every new application became a new decision.

What changed in 2023

The name moved into the ring. The monogram stayed exactly where it was, and "Gustavo Albino" now circles it. That closes the hole the first version had: a monogram on its own does not introduce you to someone who has never heard the name, and in a portfolio that is precisely who arrives first.

Colour left the logo and moved into the system. One lockup, black, with the palette working around it instead of inside it. Fewer combinations to maintain, and the brand stops changing face on every piece.

How the seal is built

The system

Six colours, with the hex values published from day one. Lemon Milk for headings, for the weight of condensed uppercase. Futura for body, for long reading.

The reference board makes the origin explicit: comics, posters, street graphics. It is not a mood board, it is a catalogue of where each decision came from.

The launch

The identity change was treated as a project, not as a profile picture swap. Thirty posts in sequence: everything new, new ID, new colours, new typeface, graphic elements, the palette with its hex values, and the logo revealed in nine parts before it appeared whole.

The proof is this page

The palette released in the 2023 campaign is the palette of this site. It was not adapted: it was already settled when the site was built, two years later.
