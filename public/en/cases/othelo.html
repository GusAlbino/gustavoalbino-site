The project

From Oliver to Othelo.

Produtora Oliver entered a new phase and became Othelo Produtora, a name that pays tribute to Grande Otelo, one of Brazil’s greatest artists. The restructure called for everything new: logotype, visual identity and digital presence, without losing the commitment to promote Brazilian culture and the diversity of its artists.

Othelo develops cultural projects and grant proposals and supports musicians, singers, visual artists, graffiti writers, painters, street artists and writers, expanding visibility, circulation and real opportunities.

Projects are approved under cultural incentive laws: Rouanet, Noemi Gontijo and Aldir Blanc, funded by MinC and the Betim city council.

As a co-founder, I led the rebrand end to end, and I remain at the company as creative director, shaping the visual language of every project.

The brand

Two vinyls become the “O”s.

The logotype comes from one simple, direct move: two vinyl records take the place of the “O”s in “Othelo”. Heavy, geometric, seventies type with record grooves drawn into the strokes: soul, black music and street culture read at a glance.

The standalone symbol, the two overlapping discs, works as the brand bug in seals, icons and short signatures.

An application system.

Positive, negative, terracotta and outline: the mark stays legible on any background, from stage to tee.

Colour & type

Graphite, orange and cream.

A warm, analogue palette: graphite and cream as the base, orange as the energy. Oswald, condensed and firm, for headlines; Montserrat for body. The brand board gathers the system, the image world and the fonts.

Website

The company, live.

I designed and built Othelo’s website to translate the brand’s cultural essence clearly and engagingly. The navigation presents artists, projects and purpose in an accessible way, strengthening the connection between artists, partners and audience.

The hero opens with the invitation “Seja grande, vem pra Othelo!”, followed by upcoming events, Instagram, the story of Grande Otelo and the mission, all in the new visual language: type, colours, graphics and information architecture aligned with the new phase.
