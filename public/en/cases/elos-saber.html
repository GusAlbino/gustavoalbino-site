About the site

Four programmes, one way in.

The home page opens with “Somos ponte” and leads to a WhatsApp conversation, where sales happen. Dark or light theme, chosen by the visitor.

Programmes

One page to choose from.

The four programmes side by side, each with its own fact sheet colour and a direct path to contact.

EOS

Teaching in higher education.

Training and mentoring for people who teach or want to enter the classroom. In person, in regular groups.

AURIS

International relations.

Training for professionals who want a wider repertoire and a clearer view of the global scene.

PROSPERA

Training for the public sector.

Strategic skills and critical thinking for people who work in public institutions.

ALÁFIA

Conscious leadership.

Consulting on human development and purpose for high-performance professionals.

On screen

From monitor to phone.

One system at three sizes. On the phone the menu becomes a button and the sheets stack into one column.
