Visual Identity · Art direction, album cover & design

Jimi Oliver

Face to Face

Visual identity and art direction for guitarist Jimi Oliver’s instrumental album, from cover to vinyl, from CD to stage. A black-and-white, photographic, elegant system worthy of his jazz-meets-Brazil fusion.

ClientJimi Oliver · Oliver Produtora
RoleArt direction, cover & graphic design
Year2024 · Betim, Brazil
DeliverablesCover, vinyl, CD, booklet & portfolio
The project

One face, one album.

Face to Face is Jimi Oliver’s album of rebirth, conceived during the pandemic, after his recovery. The music crosses Brazil’s roots and fuses with jazz; the identity had to carry the same emotional weight.

The answer was to put the artist literally face to face with the listener: a high-contrast black-and-white portrait and an extreme-contrast serif logotype. Sober, authorial and timeless. Far from any genre cliché.

The cover

Black and white, face to face.

The concept comes from the title: the musician’s face fills the whole cover in a grainy duotone, split between shadow and light. The “Face to Face” logotype, set in a Didone serif, brings the graphic shine that contrasts with the photographic grain.

That same signature repeats on every piece (vinyl, CD, booklet and stage), so the album is recognised at a glance, in any format.

The record

From vinyl to label.

The art was built for the physical object: the vinyl sleeve, the CD label with the tracks set in an arc and the Psalm 126 quote, and the open booklet with the tracklist and full credits.

Live

The cover becomes the stage.

At the launch, the same identity took over the stage: the cover portrait in giant halftone on the screen and the “JIMI” letters on LED panels. From booklet to show, one single system.

Spin-off

And it became a portfolio.

The project didn’t stop at the cover. The collaboration also produced the artist’s portfolio: an editorial document gathering Jimi Oliver’s career, albums, shows and musical résumé, with the same typographic and photographic care as the identity.

A piece ready to open doors with producers, festivals and bookers, in Brazil and abroad.

Result

An identity that plays before the first note.

From album cover to stage and portfolio, one cohesive system: black, white and high-contrast photography, with the same elegance across every touchpoint.

More than a cover: the face of an artist, ready to circulate in the instrumental-music market.
