About the project

In Defence of Animals and the Environment

Exhibition of paintings by Maria Inez Ribeiro at Casa da Cultura in Betim, on the preservation of wildlife and nature.

The artist created the project "A Arte em Defesa dos Elefantes", which became the book "Elefantes de A a Z". The poster starts there: a close-up of elephant skin in a blue to orange gradient, with the title broken across six lines that fill the full height of the sheet.

Besides the poster and the post, I designed ten thank-you cards, one for each painting on show, and the pull-up banner at the entrance. Each card carries the painting and its title, so the visitor leaves with the piece they saw.
