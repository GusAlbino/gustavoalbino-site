4th edition · 2026

Muros do Jardim Teresópolis · Cor e Vida

Urban art as a path for human development.

The project brings graffiti and urban aesthetics to the walls of Jardim Teresópolis, in Betim. Fifty students go through a training process and change how they see their own territory while learning visual technique.

The 4th edition organises the work in three themes: ethnic diversity, social and urban sustainability, and popular knowledge. Each theme becomes a street panel and later a catalogue page.

I sign the graphic design of the project. On the credits page that is the line; in practice it means the event mark and every piece that comes out of it, from the 12cm sticker to the 9 metre banner.
