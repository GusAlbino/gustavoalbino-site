About the project

Christmas Recital · Phoenix students

End-of-year recital by the students of Phoenix Escola de Música, at Loja Duetto Musical in Shopping Monte Carmo, Betim.

The identity starts from a portrait of the teacher with his guitar, red light on black, and resolves the title in handwritten lettering. The red was not my call: it is already the phoenix colour in the school logo, and the piece only amplifies what the brand had.

It shipped in three formats, each with its own crop of the portrait: a square Instagram post, an A3 poster and a 60x120cm vertical banner for the venue.
