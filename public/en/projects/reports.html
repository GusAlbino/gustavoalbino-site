Medical Cannabis Yearbook 2026

Brazilian Roots: the market passes R$ 1 billion and grows a national base.

The fifth edition closes the year the sector passed R$ 1 billion and reached R$ 1.14 billion, with over 1.1 million patients. The thread is Brazilian Roots: national cultivation, research and an industry starting to take shape inside the country.

It runs 161 pages on the new Anvisa rules, prescribers, patients, pharmacies, imports, patient associations and the company directory. I did the art direction, layout and charts.
