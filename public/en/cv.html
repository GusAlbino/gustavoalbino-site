I've been designing digital products and brands for over 10 years.

I'm the Lead Designer at Kaya, where I joined in 2022 as a designer and now own all creative work, art direction and product design. I took Kaya Doc from MVP to V2, released in April 2026. The B2B SaaS platform, live since 2024, now has over 3,000 doctors, dentists, physiotherapists and vets.

My strongest suit is design systems. The Kaya Doc one has 353 master components and two-layer tokens, built in Figma. I run the UX research and take each screen from concept to engineering handoff, on desktop, tablet and mobile.

My flow starts with brainstorming and research. I build hypotheses, turn them into a proposal, take it for approval and move on to the final version. My default is to subtract: a lean system and calculated contrast. Files go out closed, with bleed, colour space and resolution checked.

In editorial, I've laid out the sector yearbooks since 2022 and passed 50 e-books, including the Medical Cannabis Yearbook 2026 that opens this site.

The latest brand work is the Runpace rebrand, a sports eyewear brand, in 2026: guidelines, a design system and a mandatory colour ratio of 70% white, 15% blue, 10% graphite and 5% accent. I also built the site for Prof. Dr. Carolina Galdino, Elos Saber. Before those came Yerba Fina, Jimi Oliver, Hercules and Othelo, the cultural events company I co-founded in Betim.

Earlier I worked at Mindmaze in 2020 and for PelicanCorp from 2021 to 2025. I lived 8 years in Sydney and 4 and a half in Lausanne, and I've freelanced in branding since 2014. I use AI in the workflow, with skills and agents that cut repeat production, and the design decisions stay mine. I've run trail since 2018. I speak Portuguese and English fluently and French professionally, and I work remotely.
